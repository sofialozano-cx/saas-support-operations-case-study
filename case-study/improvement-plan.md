# Support Operations Improvement Plan

## Goal

Reduce time-to-detection, improve first-contact evidence quality and make multi-customer technical incidents easier to identify, escalate and communicate.

| Priority | Initiative | Owner | Expected Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | Alert on webhook queue depth and abnormal delivery latency | Engineering / Platform | Detect delivery degradation before support volume becomes the primary signal |
| P0 | Define multi-account incident trigger | Support Operations | Faster recognition of shared platform symptoms |
| P1 | Introduce webhook diagnostic intake macro | Support Operations | More complete evidence on first response |
| P1 | Standardize Engineering escalation template | Support + Engineering | Less back-and-forth during technical escalation |
| P1 | Create incident-linked customer communication workflow | Support Operations | Consistent updates across affected accounts |
| P2 | Publish webhook troubleshooting runbook | Knowledge Management / Support | Faster, repeatable investigation |
| P2 | Review webhook ticket drivers monthly | Support Operations | Identify recurring product or documentation gaps |

## Suggested Success Measures

- time from first platform-level signal to incident assessment;
- percentage of escalations containing required evidence;
- number of Engineering clarification cycles after handoff;
- percentage of incident-linked tickets receiving consistent updates;
- repeat ticket volume for known webhook troubleshooting causes.

These measures are proposed operating metrics for the fictional scenario, not reported production results.
