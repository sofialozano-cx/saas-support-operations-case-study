# Support Escalation Matrix

| Situation | Support Action | Escalation |
|---|---|---|
| Single customer, reproducible configuration issue | Troubleshoot and document | No Engineering escalation |
| Single customer, product behavior unexplained after evidence collection | Gather reproducible evidence | Technical escalation |
| Multiple customers with shared symptom | Correlate tickets and assess scope | Incident review |
| Critical workflow unavailable across customers | Declare incident based on severity criteria | Immediate Engineering / incident owner |
| Security or privacy concern | Stop normal troubleshooting and preserve evidence | Security process immediately |

## Minimum Evidence Before Standard Engineering Escalation

- customer/account context;
- business impact and urgency;
- expected vs. actual behavior;
- timestamps and identifiers;
- reproduction steps where possible;
- troubleshooting already completed;
- relevant response codes/log evidence;
- recent-change check;
- clear request to Engineering.

Incident response may require escalation before every field is complete when customer impact or severity justifies immediate action.
